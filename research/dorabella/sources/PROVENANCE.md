## Two standing cautions

**Every plate descends from the same lost original photograph.** The manuscript
disappeared after Dora Penny reproduced it in 1937. Differences between plates
bound *reproduction* noise added downstream; they cannot bound the original
photograph's own limitations. All reader-disagreement figures in this report
are therefore lower bounds on absolute error.

**The cross-edition logic that does work is asymmetric and needs no
segmentation** (§18.2a): a mark surviving an independent re-screening is on the
original; a mark appearing in one screening only is an artifact of that
screening. That is what settled the dot inventory when the full glyph-level
comparison could not be run.
