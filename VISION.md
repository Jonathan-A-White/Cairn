## Vision (draft, from his words above)
- A family app for two things: where things are, and how things are done.
- It gets what one person knows into the app, so the family need not always ask her.
- For his family first, and other families too.
- Most important: easy to put information in (AI helps) and easy to ask and get it out (AI helps).
- Lines: a photo leaves the phone only to the factory and is deleted after; never stored on an Item or Place; the AI never describes people.
